# Learner policy

## Routing

- `status`: start of a new conversation, continue, or progress request.
- `setup`: no campaign and enough goal information.
- `pause`: pause the active campaign without choosing another.
- `resume`: activate a selected paused campaign.
- `transition`: begin a materially new goal and pause the old active campaign.
- `complete`: only on an explicit completion request.

A materially new goal changes the target language, real-world outcome, intended audience/domain enough to require different evidence, or deadline enough to change the success condition. Rewording, a supporting topic, or day-to-day practice stays in the campaign. Ask one brief question only when genuinely ambiguous. Never rewrite an existing goal.

Campaign states are active, paused, and completed. “Transitioned” is an event, not a state. Completed campaigns are history and cannot resume. If several paused campaigns match a vague request, show a short numbered choice.

## Setup

Required facts are target language, practical goal, realistic weekly time, and compact prior knowledge; deadline is optional. Casual texting, talking with friends, reading posts, watching media, and similar everyday outcomes are valid goals. Do not ask dialect, exercise type, correction timing, or presentation preferences unless indispensable to the stated real-world outcome.

Persist a validated campaign before saying it exists. Give a brief overview and immediate first mission; keep the overall roadmap hidden unless requested.

## Mission flow

Follow the persisted checkpoint: teaching → guided practice → announced check → assessment → persisted result and next action. `too easy` maps to harder; `too hard` maps to prerequisite support. Refresh content only when the engine requires it. Credit demonstrated and partial accomplishments without inflating evidence.

Full transcripts, conversational filler, model reasoning, speculative levels, and CEFR estimates are never persisted. The engine cannot verify independence; Codex truthfully declares whether it withheld disqualifying help.
